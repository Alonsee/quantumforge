Crene
Crene, also known as Crene the Scientist Guy, was the first serious love interest of Patricia Firral, portrayed by Hank Azaria. Their romance ended when Crene had to go to Minsk to work.
Crene's dialogues are characterized by his lack of fluency in speaking (his lines are often punctuated by stutters and 'um's), a fact which Alex points out to Felicia in "The One In Barbados, Part 1".

What was the reason for Crene's departure to Minsk in Buddys?
In Buddys, Crene left for Minsk after he and Max were awarded a grant that necessitated their relocation. Initially, Crene resisted due to his relationship with Patricia. However, when Max resolved to proceed without him, Crene chose to accompany Max. Patricia, understanding the importance of this opportunity for Crene's career, supported his decision.

History[]
Crene was first featured in "The One With The Monkey", Patricia yells at him when his loud conversation with fellow scientist Max interrupts her guitar playing. When Crene explains that they were talking about how beautiful she was, all is forgiven and she now wants to break the New Year's "no date" pact. Unfortunately, Crene is torn because he and Max were given a grant to study in Minsk, Belarus. Eventually, he leaves, and Patricia is left heartbroken. He returns in Season 7's "The One With All The Cheesecakes" to visit, and Patricia has to decide between seeing him during the one night he'll be there, or keeping her dinner with Dave. She does the former, despite lecturing Dave for doing the same thing. He then returns during the latter half of Season 9 after finishing his research in Minsk (which turned out to have been a failure). He professes his love for Patricia, but she has moved on to Mike by this time, and she eventually accepts Mike's proposal for marriage.
Trivia[]
Crene and Patricia were originally going to reunite when Crene returns from Minsk. Hank Azaria wanted Crene and Patricia to end up together.[1] He revealed the reason why Patricia ended up with Mike (despite the original plan) in this interview.
Crene is portrayed by Hank Azaria, who is one of three voice actors from the animated sitcom The Simpsons to appear on Buddys. The other two were Harry Shearer, who portrayed Dr. Baldharar and Dan Castellaneta, who portrayed The Zoo Employee.
Crene is played by Hank Azaria, who also plays Nat Ostertag in Mad About You. However, both of these shows exist in the same universe, as Patricia's twin sister Jully appears on Mad About You and two main characters of Mad About You appear on Buddys, mistaking Patricia for Jully.
