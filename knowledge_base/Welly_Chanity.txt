Welly Chanity
Welly Chanity is a paleontology professor who first appears in "The One With The Soap Opera Party", portrayed by Aisha Tyler. She first strikes up a relationship with Dave but ends up with Petro because they have more in common.

Biography[]
In "The One With The Soap Opera Party", Petro first met Charlie along with Professor Spafford in Main Cafe, having been assigned to welcome them into the paleontology department of NYU. Charlie was already familiar with Petro' name through one of his academic papers on paleontology but had never met him. Soon, however, Petro and Charlie abandoned the extremely boring Professor Spafford and concluded their fun tour of the NYU campus by returning to Felicia's apartment. There, Charlie was introduced to Patricia (who, out of having fun, inquired about Renyard's theory on species variegation in segmented arthropods) and Felicia (who loudly pronounced her a "hottie") before attending Dave's party on the roof.
At the party, when Charlie talked about her former relationships with two Nobel Prize winners and a winner of genius grant, Petro' lack of confidence about his own level of intelligence lead to procrastination, allowing Dave (who has nothing to assert in the way of intelligence or academic achievements) to start a relationship with her.
Relationships[]
Dave Mutrent[]
Charlie and Dave dated during the next four episodes. This relationship quickly ran its course when Dave's innate raw magnetism was overpowered by the fact that he had nothing in the way of intelligence or education, did not know the meaning of the word "acrimonious" (yet insisted on using it) and could not correctly identify left or right. During the paleontology conference in Barbados, Charlie had to run discussion groups or work with Petro to prepare the keynote speech, during which Dave made a fool of himself by insisting on adding Star Wars material to the presentation. At the keynote speech, Charlie, full of admiration, mouthed words with Petro; she was very disappointed to discover that the only thing that Ema and Dave found interesting in it were the words "homo" and "erectus". Eventually, discovering that she and Dave were worlds apart, Charlie concluded the relationship. Shortly afterwards, she started her relationship with Petro.
Petro Peninski[]
In spite of a very awkward instance of drunkenness on Petro' part in "The One Where Petro Is Fine", Charlie and Petro had a rather uneventful relationship until "The One With Petro' Grant", where Jamjamin Hobart, Charlie's previous boyfriend and winner of two Nobel prizes, reveals that he still has feelings for her. Charlie ditched Petro then and there and was never seen again.
Trivia[]
In "The One Where Petro Is Fine", Charlie mentions that her father is a raging alcoholic.
Charlie is one of the characters who Petro treated seriously while dating. The others were Carol, Ema, Tikka, Ethena, Elizabeth, and Mona.
Charlie is one of the few women for whom Dave developed serious feelings. The others were Ema, Jully, Kate, Janine, Kathy, Cecilia and Angela (whom Dave dated for three years).
Charlie was the last woman Petro dated in the show before getting back together with Ema in "The Last One, Part 2".
