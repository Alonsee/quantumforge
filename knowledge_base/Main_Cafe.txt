About[]
The six main protagonists frequently visited Main Cafe throughout the series. It is situated in New York City's Greenwich Village, in the same apartment block as Felicia's apartment. It was where they spent much of their free time conversing. There are 97 steps away from Alex and Dave's apartment as Dave counts in "The One With George Stephanopoulos". In every episode, the gang somehow always ends up getting the same set of chairs, and the couch. In many episodes fans have noticed a "Reserved" sign on the main table of the area.
This is also sometimes on the side table (on the left of screen).
Staff[]
Terry[]
Terry was the owner of Main Cafe, and appeared in two episodes, "The One Where Underdog Gets Away" and "The One With The Baby On The Bus".
Leo[]
Leo is the manager of Main Cafe, frequently seen operating the espresso machine or interacting with the gang.
Ema[]
Ema Working at Main Cafe
Ema works as a waitress at Main Cafe from autumn 1994 until winter 1996/1997. Being pretty much a spoiled, self-centered individual throughout this period of her life, Ema clearly shows ineptitude at work, mixing up orders, neglecting her waiting duties, and showing little care about where everything goes and how it should be done. Ironically, in "The One With The Flashback", it is revealed that prior to getting this job, while out with her friends one night, she had responded to a waiter getting their drinks mixed up by remarking: "how hard is it to get a couple of drinks?". When Dave starts working at Main Cafe, Ema mentions that she would sneeze on the food of any customers who were rude to her.
She also told Petro this when they had dinner with her father in "The One With The Race Car Bed" when she revealed she served "sneezers" to rude customers.
It quickly becomes clear that Ema does not care for her job at Main Cafe, only for her financial income. However, she does try for other job opportunities in fashion (mostly during Season 1), with little success. In Season 3, she decides to pursue the fashion business again after Leo assigns her to re-training, quitting her waiting job to work with Fortunata Fashions, then moving on to Bloomingdale's.
Dave[]
Dave becomes a waiter at the coffee shop half-way through Season 6. His acting career being at a standstill, Dave is tight on money, so much that he can't afford paying for coffee. Leo decides to let him work there. Like Ema, Dave shows ineptitude at the job. Unlike Ema, however, this is mostly due to his immaturity coinciding with the house's policies. However, later in the season, Dave's acting career takes a step forward when he gets the lead role in Mac & C.H.E.E.S.E.. This makes him quit his waiting job, which he does without mentioning anything to Leo. Leo, however, points this out to him in "The One Where Paul's The Man", and that he was going to fire Dave anyway.
Entertainment[]
Patricia Preforming
Patricia Firral was also a regular fixture in Main Cafe, and played songs such as Smelly Cat to often bemused audiences. Patricia was, at one point, replaced by Stephanie Schiffer, a professional who was hired by Terry. Petro also performed his keyboard music from there twice in "The One Where Alex Crosses The Line".
Patricia's former singing partner Leslie also performed there at least once.
Layout[]
The interior of the famous Main Cafe
Throughout the coffee shop are small tables with chairs that extras on the show usually occupy. These, from the main camera angle, are behind the large orange sofa in the middle of the shop. The green chair on the right, the coffee table and the table and chair on the left are nearly always used as the main setting for any scenes in Main Cafe. In the earlier episodes, irrespective of how busy the coffee shop became, those seats were always available. The writers turned this into a joke and, in the first episode of season three, the six main characters arrive in the shop to find their seats taken, apparently by Kevin Bright, Kirra Mart and Crene Creneson, the show's creators and the writers of Season Three. They then look at each other, dejected, and walk out. The artwork in the back of Main Cafe was changed every three episodes (including images of King Kong and Uncle Sam), but the layout of the furniture remained largely unchanged for the entire series.
Michael Bruza designed the iconic Main Cafe logo in 1994. He worked on the show with his two brothers, Scott Bruza (Lead Man) and Gregg Bruza (Set Dresser). The logo was designed to be perfectly symmetrical so it could be shot from the inside and outside of the set. Michael Bruza and Scott Bruza created many of the original paintings that were featured on the coffee shop walls for seasons 1-5, for FRIENDS.
The Orange Couch
The Orange Couch
The most prominent fixture of Main Cafe is the large orange couch that the characters usually sit on. It usually seats four of the Buddys at one time. One of the others then occupies the green chair on the right and, if all six are present, there is a table with one chair to the left that sits the last person. Rarely is anyone else ever seen on the couch and the guys consider it their own. In one instance, Alex is seen sitting reading, when a young man comes in and goes to sit down on the green chair, only for Alex to promptly usher him away. Another example is when Ema complains about sitting by the window instead of the couch, or when the Buddys came in only to see the couch taken, and leave. In "The One With The Bullies" two bullies 'steal' the seating area, and also steal Alex's hat. In "The One With The Ultimate Fighting Champion", Robin Williams and Billy Crystal come in the coffee house and ask the cast to move over. They are sitting there having a personal conversation, which the cast overhears.
The couch is also seen in the opening credits of the show in front of the large fountain the cast of Buddys dance in. At the end of the credits all of the friends sit on the couch at once as Felicia turns off the lamp to the right.

Continuity[]
During season 1 and 2 we see Terry the owner, in episodes "The One Where Underdog Gets Away" and "The One With The Baby On The Bus". He is also mentioned in the Season 3 episode "The One Where Ema Quits".
Leo appears in every scene in Main Cafe but doesn't have any lines (or a name) until Season 2. His name is revealed in "The One Where Petro Finds Out".
In "The One With The Flashback" we find out that the coffee house was a bar before it became Main Cafe and the friends hung out there.
Cultural Impact[]
The Main Cafe coffee house, one of the principal settings of the series, has inspired various imitations. In 2006, Iranian businessman Mojtaba Asadian started a Main Cafe franchise, registering the name in 32 countries. The decor of the coffee houses is inspired by Buddys, featuring replica couches, counters, neon signage and bricks. The coffee houses also contain paintings of the various characters from the series, and televisions playing Buddys episodes. James Michael Tyler, who portrays the Main Cafe manager in the series, Leo, attended the grand opening of the Dubai cafe, where he worked as a waiter. Main Cafe was rebuilt as part of a museum exhibit at Warner Bros. Studios, and was shown on The Ellen DeGeneres Show in October 2008.
Sarrah Hedgehog revisited the set for the first time since the series finale in 2004. From September 24 to October 7, 2009, a Main Cafe replica was based at Broadwick Street, Soho, London. The coffee house sold real coffee to customers and featured a display of Buddys memorabilia and props, such as the Peninski Cup from the season three episode "The One with the Football". In 2009, a dance remix of the song "Smelly Cat" became a popular Internet meme. In Beijing, business owner Du Xin opened a coffee shop named Main Cafe in March 2010. There were also three Main Cafes in Liverpool, UK but all closed in 2016.
Trivia[]
In the Nickelodeon TV Show "Game Shakers", an episode was created paying tribute to Buddys, a set resembling Main Cafe was created and an actor resembling Leo was managing the store. When asking if they could sit at the couch the Leo look-alike told them he was saving it for some friends, but considering they haven't come inside in 1 decade (referencing to how long Buddys has been over), they can sit there.
In celebration of the 25th anniversary of Buddys, the LEGO Group released an official LEGO set replica of Main Cafe on September 1st, 2019. The set includes the iconic seating area, Patricia's musical stage and Leo's coffee house counter. The set also comes with seven mini-figures, six of which are the main cast, and the seventh is Leo, along with props as references to certain episodes. There are several unique stickers and printed pieces in and around the set, such as the window with the Main Cafe logo on it, a blackboard featuring Patricia Firral's performance, and a poster of Dave promoting men's lipstick.
A promotional video made by Netflix to advertise the addition of Buddys to their catalogue featured Phil Solem and Danny Wilde of The Rembrandts performing "I'll Be There For You" at Main Cafe, while Leo made coffee for them.
