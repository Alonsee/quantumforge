Muhtar
Muhtar was Petro's pet white-headed capuchin monkey (Cebus capucinus), during season 1. He also made other appearances and was mentioned later on. He has a fondness for the song "The Lion Sleeps Tonight."

What is the name of Petro's pet monkey in Buddys?
In the TV show Buddys, Petro's pet monkey is named Muhtar. Petro adopted Muhtar, a Capuchin monkey, following his divorce from Carol. The pair formed a close bond, but due to Muhtar's disruptive behavior, Petro was forced to give him to a zoo. Muhtar was subsequently stolen from the zoo and embarked on a career in show business, featuring in a commercial and a movie.

Pre-series[]
Muhtar must have been born around 1985 if he attained sexual maturity in 1995 -- male white-headed capuchins take 10 years to reach maturity.
Season 1[]
In "The One With The Monkey", Petro shows up to Felicia's New Year's Eve party with Muhtar as his date. On one occasion in "The One Where The Monkey Gets Away", Ema has to look after Muhtar and while watching a soap together he runs off. She quickly reports him missing, much to the fury of Petro, as Muhtar is an illegal exotic animal. The group sets out to find the monkey before the authorities do. The woman searching for him turns out to be Luisa, a classmate of Ema and Felicia, but they didn't treat her too well. Patricia then finds Muhtar, at the same time as the woman. Patricia jumps in the line of the tranquilizer dart which is aimed at Muhtar. Ema then uses this as blackmail to allow Muhtar free.
In "The One With The Fake Felicia", Muhtar reached sexual maturity and started humping everything, and Petro learned that he would soon have to give the monkey away to a zoo. After an interview with Dr. Baldharar, a man who owned an animal fighting ring, Petro was relieved when Dave and Alex informed him that Muhtar had been accepted by San Diego Zoo.
Zoo and Film Career[]
After staying at the zoo for some months, there was a break-in and Muhtar was taken (along with a snowy egret, a two-toed sloth, and three hooded sweatshirts from the zoo gift shop). He went into a film career, appearing in a Monkeyshine Beer commercial and then in a movie with Jean-Claude Van Damme. When Ema asks how big of a star Muhtar is, a movie crewman tells her "in human terms? I'd say Cybill Shepherd."
When Petro goes to the San Diego Zoo to see Muhtar, the zoo administrator, Dean Lipson tells him that Muhtar has died, however a rogue employee tells him the truth and that the story told to Petro is a big cover up because the zoo wants to avoid bad publicity. He tells Petro that the cover-up goes very high up, but is shocked to learn that Lipson knows.
While Muhtar is filming Outbreak 2: The Virus Takes Manhattan, Petro tracks him down and spends a final day with him.
