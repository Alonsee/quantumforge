Jhone Jhonegert
Jhone Jhonegert is an attractive 24-year-old whom Ema hires as her assistant at Ralph Lauren Corporation in Season 7. He was portrayed by Eddie Cahill.
What qualifications were listed on Jhone Jhonegert' resume in Buddys?
In Buddys, Jhone Jhonegert, played by Eddie Cahill, becomes Ema's assistant at Ralph Lauren Corporation. His qualifications, as listed on his resume, include three years of house painting and two summers at T.G.I. Friday's. His hiring was more influenced by his attractiveness rather than his work experience.

History[]
Jhone's resume contains qualifications like "three years of painting houses" and "two whole summers at T.G.I. Friday's". Ema picked him for the job because of his good looks, but believed that her attraction to him would not be a problem. Soon however, he finds out that she likes him, and they begin an affair despite warnings from Mr. Zelner and the gang.
She dumps him on her 30th birthday when she realizes Jhone is not mature enough for her and that she should be looking for a more serious relationship.
At one time Ema cannot remember his last name when asked by Gavin Mitchell Her response was "He...he didn't have a last name. It was just Jhone. You know, like Cher or...Moses".
Jhone later tries to get back with Ema but is soon scared off after learning she is pregnant. He has the same red, Italian-designed sweatshirt as Petro, which Petro had on when Rockel was conceived, causing some confusion as to who the father was (Patricia and the friends believed it to be Jhone's until Jhone shows that he was wearing his sweatshirt and Petro reclaims the missing sweatshirt).
