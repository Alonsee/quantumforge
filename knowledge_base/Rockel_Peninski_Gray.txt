Rockel Peninski-Gray
Rockel Peninski-Gray is the daughter of Petro Peninski and Ema Gray. Ema's pregnancy was originally revealed in the season finale of Season 7.
Rockel is born in "The One Where Ema Has A Baby, Part 2". Rockel lives with both her parents after she is born, until they get into a fight over Petro not telling Ema that a man that she met at a bar left a message for her. Ema then moves back in with Dave. In the series finale her parents reconcile and get back together.

Birth and Early Life[]
Rockel was born on April 4, 2002, after 47 hours and 27 minutes of labor, to Petro and Ema, though just before Ema was taken into the delivery room, the doctor said Ema had been in labor for 21 hours. It's possible that the time after Rockel's birth was included when Alex said that it was good to see Rockel after 47 hours of labor (said to Ema). (In most cases, a baby in her situation would have been born via emergency C-section). She was born in "The One Where Ema Has A Baby, Part 2". She was born breech (buttocks first).
Rockel is the result of a one-night stand, as shown in "The One With The Videotape". Petro and Ema move in together during her pregnancy so Petro could be more involved with the baby and be there for Ema. After Rockel's birth, they all live together until the season 9 episode "The One Where Felicia Sings". Petro and Ema get into a fight when Ema kisses her colleague Gavin, and Petro brings another woman into the apartment. Realizing their living arrangement isn't working anymore, and Ema and Rockel move in with Dave again.
At Rockel's first birthday party, Ema orders a cake shaped like a bunny, but it ends up looking like a man's genitals. While they go back to the bakery to fix it, Alex and Felicia babysit. During this time Alex accidentally teaches Rockel something. When everyone returns, Rockel holds up her finger when Alex asks how old she is. Petro and Ema are especially happy with this milestone, with Petro exclaiming that she can go to science camp, which Ema disagrees with.
While Ema's sister Any acts as a babysitter, she gets Rockel's ears pierced, much to both Ema and Petro' horror, since she did not inform them about her plan. Any also states that Rockel is ugly and dull.
Although Rockel enjoys swings, Ema doesn't like her daughter using swings because of an accident when Ema was four. The accident was that her hair got caught in the swing's chain, and in order for her to escape, her mother had to cut a lump out of her hair, and it was uneven for weeks.
In "The One With The Late Thanksgiving", Patricia gets Ema to secretly enter Rockel in a baby beauty pageant. Rockel wins the pageant and $1,000.
In the series finale of Buddys, Ema and Petro get back together, bringing them closer to becoming a happy family. Early in her life, Rockel seemed to enjoy the song "Baby got Back", which was the only way her parents could get her to laugh in her early stages. Later on, Rockel also enjoyed going on swings.
Name[]
Her family name is "Peninski-Gray". When Rockel was born, Ema insisted her last name be Peninski-Gray, after the nurses originally said just Gray.
The name Rockel was originally chosen by Felicia as the name she wanted to use for her future daughter, but once Ema heard the name and loved it so much, Felicia let her have it. Before deciding on "Rockel", Petro and Ema discussed several other names like "Delilah", (nixed because Ema felt it made her sound like "a Biblical whore") "Isabella" (eliminated because the name didn't 'feel right'), and "Ruth" (which was eliminated because of the candy 'Baby Ruth' after Ema saying "We'll have ourselves a little Baby Ruth...".)
Ironically, in the season 2 episode "The One Where Old Yeller Dies", when Petro and Ema were dating, he states he already picked out the name of their future daughter - "Ethena", which later turned out to be the name of his second ex-wife. Ema's sister, Any, also mistakenly refers to her as "Ethena", and also as "Ella". At first she even calls her "Emmett", when she mistakes her for a boy.
Family[]
Rockel's parents are Ema Gray and Petro Peninski. She has two aunts on her mothers' side, Aunt Jill and Aunt Any. On her fathers' side, she has her Aunt Felicia and her Uncle Alex (Alex is related to her via marriage). Rockel's grandparents are Sandra and James Gray (divorced) and Judy and Charlie Peninski. Rockel has an older half-brother, Jam, with whom she shares a father. Jam's mother is Petro's first wife, Carol (who split with Petro because she was a lesbian). Rockel also has an Aunt Patricia and an Uncle Dave, but they are both not related to her in any way.
Her adopted cousins are Charlie and Erica Peninski-Callecher, twin son and daughter of her aunt and uncle, Alex and Felicia. Their first daughter was supposed to be named 'Rockel', but Felicia gave the name to Ema after baby Rockel was born. Felicia and Alex named their own daughter Erica after the twins' biological mother, and their son after Charlie, Felicia and Petro' father.

Parents[]
Rockel's mother and father
Petro and Ema loved their daughter very much. They were always protective of her and always made sure she was looked after correctly.
Extended family and friends[]
Felicia, Alex, Charlie and Erica, her paternal aunt, uncle and cousins
Through Rockel's family on her father's side, she has her grandparents Charlie and Judy, Aunt Felicia (related by blood), Uncle Alex (related by marriage; Felicia's husband) (2 of the main characters), half-brother Jam Peninski, and her cousins Charlie and Erica. Charlie was fond of Rockel, as well as Judy obviously was. Felicia loved Rockel very much, especially since she was the daughter of her best friend and brother (something she deeply desired) and Alex also loved her: They even got along very well together and even babysat her one night and would even be her guardians if Petro and Ema died (Although Alex had to prove himself as an independent authority figure when he learned that he would initially only get Rockel if Felicia was alive). Her relationship with her half-brother and cousins is unknown, but it's likely it would later turn out be a loving one.
Through Rockel's family on her mother's side, she has her grandparents James and Sandra, Aunt Jill (related by blood) and Aunt Any (related by blood) (2 of the main characters). James may not have been too fond of her because of her being illegitimate or he was not allowed near her because Petro and Ema knew he would be a Very bad influence on her, while Sandra obviously loved her and usually babysat her. Jill was likely fond of her niece, while Any on the other hand was obviously very not, being completely useless when entrusted with Rockel after a last-minute cancellation by her mother.

Trivia[]
Rockel was conceived the 299th time Petro and Ema had sex. (In "The One With The Cop", Petro reveals that they have had sex 298 times to that point. In "The One After Vegas", both Ema and Petro denied having had sex the night before. Out of the 300 times they had sex over the series' run, including the final time in the series finale, Rockel's conception missed the 300th by one time.) There is a hint that they had sex after getting drunkenly married in Las Vegas which would make it the 300th time.  There are hints that they had sex at other times too.
Rockel's favorite song is Baby Got Back. It makes her laugh. Her parents are seen singing it to her by the other friends.
Rockel's first word is "gleba", which is the "fleshy, spore-bearing inner mass of a certain fungi". At first Petro does not think this counts as her first word, as he does not think it is a real one, but Ema finds it in the dictionary. He becomes excited by this, proclaiming "she's gonna be a scientist!".
She never shares a scene with her half-brother Jam Peninski throughout the series.
There is debate over whether Ema's pregnancy started on April or July/August 2001:
In "The One With The Videotape", we learn Petro and Ema had sex little more than a month before Felicia and Alex's wedding
Evidence supporting Ema's pregnancy starting in April:
It is first revealed that Ema is pregnant in the season finale of Season 7 (which was aired in May 2001), with Ema being about a month pregnant. If this were true, Rockel would have been born in January 2002.
In the episode "The One With The Cheap Wedding Dress" Felicia tells another bride she is getting married on the 15th of May, meaning that Ema is pregnant in May. It is possible that the wedding date was moved to September after the events of this episode.
Evidence supporting Ema's pregnancy starting in July/August:
In the white Buddys box set there is a book that shows a prop of Rockel's name tag, stating she was born on April 4, 2002. This would make sense if you Felicia and Alex's wedding as taking place at the Season 8 premiere (which was aired in September 2001).
In the episode "The One With Petro' Step Forward", aired in December, Patricia states that Ema is 4 months pregnant.
In "The One With Ema's Date", aired in October Petro says Ema is 2 months pregnant. Again, this is correct when Ema's pregnancy started in August 2001.
In "The One With the Stripper", Felicia tells Alex he should consider the stripper as his two-months anniversary present. This episode takes place in November, after the Halloween episode, suggesting the wedding took place in September.
Petro stated once that he would like to name his daughter Ethena, but it never happened, likely due to his failed marriage 4 years before Rockel's birth with a British woman named Ethena.
Rockel was portrayed, though uncredited, by Cali and Noelle Sheldon, who were actually born a month after Rockel on June 17, 2002. In a few episodes, Cali and Noelle had started talking, which caused problems during setting. This was similar to Jam Peninski, Petro' other child.
Rockel would be 22–23 as of 2025.
In the bloopers of the episode "The One Where Ema's Sister Baby-Sits", when Any states that her boyfriend is Mark's dad, Rockel starts laughing with the crowd causing the rest of the actors to laugh as well.
In "The One With The Cake", it's revealed that Petro wants to send her to science camp when she is older.
Petro said in "The One Where Old Yeller Dies" that he and Ema would have two children and the first would be a girl, which basically foreshadowed Rockel's birth and it was hinted that they had another child after the series ended.
Rockel's also foreshadowed 4 years prior to her birth, in the season 4 episode, "The One With Patricia's Uterus", when Ema says she would have a baby with someone she loves and the baby would be a keeper. Rockel is then revealed to be Petro' baby in "The One With The Red Sweater".
Ema's future pregnancy is further hinted at in "The One Hundredth" when Patricia tells Ema she won't be able to stand the pain when Ema asked if giving birth hurt. Rockel's birth proves to be longer and more painful.
In "The One Where Ema's Sister Baby-Sits", it's hinted that she will have a younger sister when Ema comments that she likes the name 'Ella' and Petro tells Ema "fine we'll call the next one Ella".
Her western zodiac sign is Aries and her Chinese zodiac sign is a Horse.
