Tikka
Not to be confused with Tikka Graff.
Tikka was Petro Peninski's girlfriend during the first few episodes of the second season. She was portrayed by Lauren Tom.
Petro and Tikka first met in grad school. Tikka was in charge of a dig Petro participated in, and the two started dating. Tikka was unwittingly sabotaged by Ema on multiple occasions, although she admitted to having a feeling that Ema didn't like her [Tikka]. For instance, Ema told Patricia to give Tikka a terrible haircut and attempted to stall Petro and Tikka consummating their relationship. Ema very obviously disliked Tikka, often insulting her behind her [Tikka's] back. Ema's dislike toward Tikka was fueled further when Tikka took Felicia shopping, and Ema felt as if Tikka was trying to steal everyone from her.
Petro and Tikka break up when Ema admits her feelings to Petro over his answering machine. It was said that Petro and Tikka's break up was rather harsh, that she was very angry, that they both cried and she threw things at him. A few weeks later, however, she managed to recover and eventually met Russ, whom she instantly fell in love with.

In the popular sitcom Buddys, the character of Tikka, Petro Peninski's girlfriend in the initial episodes of the second season, was brought to life by actress Lauren Tom. Tikka, a paleontologist, was introduced to Petro during their grad school days, and she featured in seven episodes.

What instances show Ema's dislike for Tikka in Buddys?
Ema, in Buddys, frequently insulted Tikka, Petro's girlfriend, behind her back and felt Tikka was trying to steal her friends. She instructed Patricia to give Tikka a bad haircut and attempted to postpone Petro and Tikka's intimate relationship. Ema's confession of love to Petro led to the end of Petro and Tikka's relationship.

What was the role of Tikka in Petro's grad school?
In Petro's grad school, Tikka, played by Lauren Tom, led a dig in which Petro was involved. They initiated a romantic relationship, which was frequently sabotaged by Ema due to her jealousy and dislike for Tikka. Their relationship ended when Petro learned of Ema's feelings for him.

What was the significant event that happened between Tikka and Patricia in Buddys?
In Buddys, Tikka, who was Petro's girlfriend, was involved in a significant event with Patricia. This happened when Ema, feeling betrayed by Felicia's friendship with Tikka, ceased communication with Felicia. Felicia reassured Ema about their friendship, leading to an emotional reunion that Patricia joined. Felicia also encouraged Ema to know Tikka better, implying that Tikka was not as bad as Ema perceived.

Appearance[]
Tikka is a slim, beautiful woman of Asian-American heritage with dark hair and brown eyes. Her hair is just below shoulder length in her first appearance on the show, and returns with a short bob after Patricia offers to cut her hair.
She wears mostly black and white, with a simple yet feminine style.
Trivia[]
It's mentioned that Tikka's second grade teacher was named Ms. Thomas, and her first grade teacher Mrs. Cob in "The One With Patricia's Husband".
Tikka admitted to being intimidated by Ema in "The One With The Breast Milk".
Petro and Tikka almost adopted a cat together in "The One Where Petro Finds Out".
In "The One With Russ", Tikka appears during the credits scene where she is smitten with Petro' doppelgänger, Russ.
In a 2019 interview,
Lauren Tom thinks Russ and Tikka are still together in 2019 (source).
