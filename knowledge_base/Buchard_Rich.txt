Buchard Rich
Buchard Rich, M.D. is an ophthalmologist who is close friends with Felicia and Petro' parents. He is twenty-one years older than Felicia and has a romantic relationship with her during Season 2 of Buddys. He was portrayed by Tom Selleck.

Character[]
Buchard Rich mentions in "The One With The Chicken Pox" that he grew up in California. Prior to any of his appearances on the show, he was married to his high school sweetheart Barbara for many years and had two children, Michelle and Timothy Rich. At some point, he also met and became good friends with Charlie and Judy Peninski. Barbara and Buchard eventually divorced, but the reason behind their divorce is never revealed. Buchard is also the grandfather of his daughter's son, Henry.
Series[]
Season 2[]
Buchard's first onscreen appearance is in "The One Where Petro And Ema...You Know". Felicia and Patricia cater for a party he has thrown, and his reaction upon seeing how thin she is suggests that prior to this it had been years since he last saw her. Throughout the party, he seems to enjoy conversing with Felicia more than attending to his party guests, all ophthalmologists. Patricia and Felicia discuss this while he is not in the kitchen. Felicia seems attracted to him, even though they are 21 years apart in age. (Patricia says Felicia is "so much the smitten kitten.") She tries to deny it, but she is caught when revealing she has an eye appointment with him in spite of just having her eyes checked. At Felicia's eye appointment, she and Buchard try to maintain a platonic relationship with each other, but they give in and kiss passionately. Felicia and Ema prepare for their respective dates. At the guy's apartment (where Patricia has now been sucked into the do-nothing-but-watch-TV crowd with Dave and Alex), Felicia gets grilled by Petro about her date. He does his best not to overreact to the fact that she's dating their father's best friend. He finds it hard to believe that Felicia finds Dr. Rich sexy; an opinion both Ema and Patricia definitely agree to. This gets interrupted by a call from the museum. Petro has to go and fix several displays that were organized incorrectly. Following Felicia and Buchard's date, we find out that Buchard is a grandfather twice over. His daughter, Michelle, is an old high school friend of Felicia's. They realize the great difference in their ages and decide not to pursue a relationship. But when they start making out again, they figure they can take their time with it.
("The One Where Petro and Ema... You Know")
Felicia and Petro are celebrating their father's birthday, to which Buchard is invited. At the party, however, there's only one subject going on - the "twinkie from the City" Buchard has managed to hook up with. The women judge her as being dumb and young enough to have "everything pointing up". Buchard is a hero with his guys for managing to end up with a young hot girl. No one's aware that Felicia is the twinkie. Felicia and Buchard meet in the bathroom, where they decide to tell everyone everything. Buchard goes out first, but Felicia doesn't leave in time and is stuck in the bathroom with her mother and father, who don't know she's there. Judy and Charlie discuss Buchard and the twinkie. Charlie admits to Judy how Buchard's very happy with his new girl, and how he's thinking he's falling in love with her. Not much later, Charlie is feeling up Judy and Felicia has to bear the whole scene. Later, Felicia tells her mother about Buchard, which by the look on Judy's face, does not go down well. She and Charlie don't have much time to mull it over, however, as everyone bring Charlie the birthday cake. ("The One Where Dave Moves Out")
Patricia finds new couple Felicia and Buchard very cute, and makes a short speech to him about how she likes him the most out of all the boyfriends Felicia has had (which were "a lot"). Buchard and Felicia pursue the matter at her apartment, where he reveals that he has only slept with two women during his whole life (Felicia included). Felicia finds this weird, but quickly changes her mind when she realizes that he has only slept with women he's been in love with. ("The One Where Dr. Ramoray Dies")
Felicia makes Buchard, Petro, and Ema watch Old Yeller with her. When Dave and Alex have an extra ticket for a Knicks game, Felicia convinces them to take Buchard, but they do it only because they can go with his Jaguar. Dave and Alex start really liking him, so much that Alex starts growing a mustache and Dave starts smoking cigars. During a foosball game, Dave accidentally let it slip that they see Buchard more as a dad than as a friend, which really hurts Buchard. ("The One Where Old Yeller Dies")
Felicia is upset that Buchard doesn't have one little thing that he is obsessive about until he finally finds something to acquiesce her: that he has to sleep on a certain side of the bed. ("The One With The Chicken Pox") Felicia asks Buchard about their future plans. Buchard sees themselves in Paris, a happy couple without kids. Felicia, however, is keen on having children of her own, but doesn't pursue the subject as she fears it would become uncomfortable. At Barry and Mindy's wedding, she and Buchard tackle the subject again; however, neither of them has shifted position from before. While dancing, Buchard offers to have kids with Felicia if he absolutely has to because he doesn't want to lose her. While excited about it at first, Felicia realized that she wants to have a baby with someone who really wants to, not just has to. The two are devastated and break up. ("The One With Barry and Mindy's Wedding")
Season 3[]
It takes Felicia months to get over the break-up, only to bump into Buchard (now sans moustache) at the video store. The two have a pleasant conversation and then go grab a bite. He then asks her to help him cook a lasagna, which she does. The two know that there is sexual tension between them, but don't act upon it - until Buchard starts calling Felicia on her phone from behind her apartment door. The two re-unite - casually, it seems - but Felicia starts taking a liking to Buchard as a boyfriend again. Although she doesn't want him to cancel on his blind date, Felicia sets herself up in Buchard's apartment like she did last time - waiting for him on a bed of roses with a rose bud between her teeth. When he shows up with the blind date he was supposed to be with on a different night, she panics. He sees her before letting his blind date in and finds a way of getting rid of his new girl. When he talks about things to Felicia, she realizes that she's not fine with the fling thing, and proposes to Buchard that they get together again. He reminds her that while they could get back together, they would still be facing the same issues that caused them to break up in the first place, so the two agree for their own sake not to keep seeing each other anymore, but decide to have one last night of passion. ("The One Where Felicia And Buchard Are Just Buddys")
Season 5[]
Felicia, who is now dating Alex, tells Patricia and Ema that she ran into Buchard and they had lunch and that she now feels nothing for him. When Alex finds out during the group's trip to Las Vegas, the two have a big fight. Once they reconcile, they decide to get married in the Season Finale (during the Season 6 premiere, they get cold feet and decide to just move in together instead). ("The One In Vegas, Part 1"/"The One In Vegas, Part 2")
Season 6[]
Buchard makes his final appearance, interfering with Alex's plans to propose to Felicia, stating that he is finally willing to get married and have children. He backs down after realizing that Alex is prepared to offer her the same thing, and that they are happy together. ("The One With The Proposal, Part 1"/"The One With The Proposal, Part 2")
Season 9[]
After Dave decides to invest his money, Felicia suggests real estate after hearing from her father that Buchard's apartment is for sale. Alex accompanied him there, but Buchard was not home, and the realtor told them that he was buying a much larger apartment, with a view of Central Park. Alex and Dave also find and take a tape with Felicia's name on it, and though Buchard had recorded over the original footage with video of him with another woman, Felicia confirms that the tape originally of her and Buchard having sex.
