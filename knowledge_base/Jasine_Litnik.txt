Jasine Litnik
Jasine Litnik (née Hosenstein, previously Litman) is a recurring character of Buddys. She was Alex Callecher's long-time on-off girlfriend. She is known for her obnoxious and nasally voice and her trademark cry of "OH... MY... GOD!" She is portrayed by Maggie Wheeler.

The character Jasine from Buddys is fully known as Jasine Litnik. Her maiden name was Hosenstein. After her first marriage to Gary Litman, she was known as Jasine Litman. Following her second marriage to Sid Goralnik, she became Jasine Goralnik.

What is Jasine's trademark cry in the Buddys series?
In the Buddys series, Jasine Litnik's trademark is her catchphrase, 'Ohhhh. Myyy. Gaawd!', typically used upon her entrance. Delivered in a nasal voice with a thick New York accent, this phrase is a running gag and a defining characteristic of her character.

Why does Alex Callecher keep resuming his relationship with Jasine?
Alex Callecher's recurring relationship with Jasine Litnik stems from his commitment-phobia and guilt. When Jasine cries during break-up attempts, Alex's guilt prompts him to continue the relationship. His need for companionship, particularly on occasions like New Year's Eve, also leads him back to Jasine. Despite finding her character traits annoying, Alex is repeatedly drawn to Jasine.

Biography[]
Season 1[]
Jasine is the girlfriend of Alex Callecher since before the start of season one. Alex wants to break up with Jasine, but every time he tries, she bursts into tears, and he ends up resuming his relationship with her out of guilt. He even ends up poking her in the eye by accident while making a hand gesture in the midst of yet another unsuccessful attempt to break up with her. After learning how good Patricia is at breaking up with people without upsetting them in the process, however, he asks her to break up with Jasine on his behalf. This she does, with total ease. ("The One With The East German Laundry Detergent")
On New Years Eve, the six friends make a pact to not have a date on New Years Day, but Alex panics when Felicia gets a date, so he calls up Jasine to get back together with her so they can kiss after the countdown to New Years Day. Alex finds Jasine really annoying during their time together at Felicia's apartment for the party. Alex ends up breaking up with Jasine again before New Years Day which leaves her upset. ("The One With The Monkey")
Alex and Jasine wake up after a drunken Valentines Day
With Valentine's Day coming up, Alex doesn't have a date and Dave does. Dave has his date Lorraine set Alex up on a blind date with one of her friends. At the restaurant, Alex's date turns out to be none other than Jasine, who is still angry with him for breaking up with her twice in several months. They are put in an awkward situation at dinner and are soon abandoned by Dave and Lorraine. To Alex's surprise, he and Jasine end up having a good time together and find that they enjoy buying expensive liquor while using Dave's credit card to pay for it. The following morning, Alex and Jasine wake up in bed together and a horrified Alex breaks up with her, yet again. To his surprise, Jasine takes it well, saying that she knows that they'll end up back together one day. ("The One With The Candy Hearts")
Season 2[]
After the death of Mr. Heckles, who leaves all of his possessions to Ema and Felicia, Alex, upon discovering Heckles' diary, is disturbed to learn that when Heckles was Alex's age, his life virtually mirrored Alex's life at the present time. Realizing that Heckles' refusal to commit himself to the people around him is the reason why he died alone, Alex, who at this point in the series has a similar attitude towards the people around him, becomes paranoid that he will end up dying alone just like Heckles did. He decides to attempt to commit himself to other people more, and calls Jasine in order to rekindle his relationship with her and attempt some sort of genuine commitment with her this time. Much to his surprise, however, it turns out Jasine is now married and pregnant with a baby girl, explaining that she hadn't informed him of this over the telephone in order to see the look on his face when he learned upon seeing her that she was pregnant, knowing it would amuse her. Jasine leaves the coffee house after that. ("The One Where Heckles Dies")
Alex and Jasine reunite
Alex joins a dating website and starts talking to a woman whom he gets along with really well. Alex continues talking to the woman and learns that she is married and her husband is cheating on her. The woman wants to meet Alex, and Patricia, being present at the moment, suggests that he actually should meet her. The next day, Alex is left waiting a long time for the woman to arrive and he begins to make the assumption that she has stood him up. In the closing moments in the season two finale, Jasine walks into Main Cafe, and upon seeing Alex, exclaims her catchphrase "OH MY GOD!!!" ("The One With Barry And Mindy's Wedding")
Season 3[]
Alex and Jasine have rekindled their relationship, to the chagrin of Dave, who finds Jasine insufferable. He tries to talk to the group about how to get rid of her, but they seem to have accepted her into the group more easily than he has. Eventually, Alex learns that Dave has a problem with Jasine and tells her about this. Jasine talks to Dave about this and hangs out with him for a whole day, making multiple attempts to get him to like her. Although Dave pretends to have had fun, in reality he still finds Jasine insufferable, but Alex appreciates his gesture and his efforts to like her. ("The One With The Princess Leia Fantasy")

Personality[]
Jasine is portrayed as a drama queen with an unbearably annoying nasal cackle. She is also apparently rather shallow and self-absorbed, since she seems to be completely blind to the fact that the friends find her unbearably irritating — no matter how hard the six of them find it to endure having to listen to the sound of her voice, Jasine shows almost no awareness whatsoever of the fact that they feel this way and ignorantly proceeds to annoy them further.
Her trademark expression is "OH! MY! GOD!" with noticeable pauses in between each word and hands flapping in sync. Some speculate that she is a parody of Fran Drescher, known for her role in The Nanny [Although they do only sound similar and both have big hair]. Even though her first and second appearance had made her seem like a nice lady in love with Alex, the third got to the point where she became annoying to the whole gang, including Alex. Ironically and to their chagrin, she seems to enjoy spending time with the six of them, presumably because she finds their wacky, quirky, goofy personalities amusing, which is naturally a huge inconvenience to them most of the time. She does, however, support Ema as the latter is giving birth and console Alex while he is nervous about his fertility tests, thus showing that she nonetheless remains a sympathetic person with a kind heart. The six friends seem to be aware of this and always seem to care about Jasine on some level despite the fact that they can't stand her, since no matter how desperate they are to get rid of her whenever they have the misfortune of encountering her, they always seem to rule out any methods of doing so which would hurt her feelings in the process, and never seem to consider truthfully telling her the way they feel about her. Her good nature was indirectly used against her when Alex and Felicia were attempting to avoid having her attending their wedding and moving in next door to them; they got her to leave by claiming that Alex still had feelings for her, reasoning that Jasine wouldn't stick around if she believed she would jeopardize their relationship.

Trivia[]
Maggie Wheeler's portrayal of Jasine is a parody of Fran Drescher, who stars in The Nanny.
In some versions of the subtitles, it is stated that Jasine's laughter is "machine-gun-fire-like".
If the number of times Alex slept with his girlfriends during the course of the show is proportional to their appearances, then Jasine is the girl whom he slept with most after Felicia.
Except for "The One With The East German Laundry Detergent", "The One With The Monkey", "The One With The Metaphorical Tunnel", "The One With Alex's Work Laugh" and "The One With Unagi" (Where she only 'appeared' on a mixtape), Jasine's first words on-screen are always: "OH! MY! GOD!".
The first name of Jasine's daughter with Gary Litman is never revealed. Her surname is assumed to also be Litman.
Maggie Wheeler originally auditioned for the part of Felicia but was turned down and given the role of Jasine instead.
Jasine has had sexual relations with both Alex and Petro, as known in "The One With Alex's Work Laugh".
If her voice appearance in Season 6 is taken into account, this would make her one of the few non-main characters to appear in all 10 seasons; next to Leo and Felicia and Petro' parents, Charlie and Judy.
In a 2019 interview, Maggie Wheeler thought Jasine has started her own leopard-print clothing line and has a relationship-advice blog (source).
