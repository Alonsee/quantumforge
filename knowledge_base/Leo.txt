Leo
Leo is the manager of Main Cafe, where the six main characters spent a great deal of their free time. He is portrayed by the late James Michael Tyler.

Character[]
Leo has a smoking habit in common with Alex.[1] Since season 3, he has had a consistent unrequited love for Ema but only gets the courage to confess in the series finale, when he finds out that she is going to Paris. Leo used to be an actor, having played a character called Bryce in All My Children. Being killed off by getting buried in an avalanche is how he ends up being a waiter at Main Cafe. He confesses this to Dave in "The One Where Eddie Won't Go", after Dave confides in him that his character, Dr. Drake Ramoray, was thrown down a shaft in daytime show Days of our Lives.
Though Leo is a permanent fixture in the everyday lives of the friends, he never penetrates the inner circle. This becomes a running joke. At Petro' bachelor party, Alex announces Leo as his best man, to which Leo instantly replies: "What's my last name?". Alex defeatedly answers "Main Cafe?". Another example of this is when Petro is celebrating getting tenure. Petro asks Leo for six glasses, to which Leo asks hopefully "You want me to join you?". Petro replies "Oh, I thought Dave was here - five is good."
Leo is on good enough terms with the friends, however, to often be invited to their informal parties, such as Patricia's birthday party, Ema's birthday party, Ethena's farewell party, Petro' bachelor party, Ema's premature surprise birthday party and Alex and Felicia's Halloween party. He is, however, left out of the official formalities, like Petro' London wedding and Felicia and Alex's wedding, but he attends Patricia's wedding.
In a 2019 interview, James Michael Tyler said he thought Leo would be the owner of Main Cafe and a very small apartment in either the East or West Village, but is content with his life.
Appearance[]
Leo's peroxide blonde hair is a source of much entertainment. Ema describes it as being "as bright as the sun," and when Leo tells Dave he has an appointment to have it dyed, Dave says: "But I love your natural color." When guessing the identity of the father of Ema's baby, Patricia asks: "Is it Leo?" Ema denies this, to which Patricia says: "Thank God, because that hair on a baby?"
Leo is commonly used as an unattractive alternative to the main friends, but confusingly, is sometimes seen as somewhat of a sex symbol towards the end of the show. In Season 5, when helping Ema get over Petro, Felicia includes Leo in the list of prospective dates for Ema, which he overhears and shows off his posture in reply. Ema begins to say "I guess he is kind of cute" only to be thrown off course by an old flirt of hers, which Leo identifies and throws out of Main Cafe. Petro and Felicia's mother, Judy, remarks how Leo is a "sexy blond" and prominently adds him to her "list" of people she can sleep with without cheating.
Patricia also gives small impressions that she may secretly be attracted to Leo, despite often mocking him before ("That hair on a baby!"). She once remarked "Sure, Leo is sexy in an obvious way...", and when referring to the number of attractive men in Paris, says that "it's like a city of Leos". She also does not hesitate at all to kiss Leo in "The One With Dave's New Girlfriend", although her main objective is that of catching a cold, and she does not kiss him until she hears him sneeze.
Ema[]
In Season 3 it emerged[2] that Leo had a crush on Ema. He often fantasized about marrying her and let it slip to Dave that one of the perks of his job was being able to stare at her all day. He was jealous of any other men who got into a relationship with her, including Petro and at Petro' bachelor party before his marriage to Ethena Eltham he thanks him for not marrying Ema and on a different night, he whispers to Ethena that "Ema is my girlfriend".
Leo would give Ema anything she wants. This is shown, for instance, in "The One With The Joke", Ema was angry with Leo for firing Dave after Dave left work to go to an audition, even though Dave was new and shouldn't have been left in charge. She then demanded that Leo give Dave his job back, which he immediately agreed to, without question, showing that all Ema has to do to get something from Leo is ask for it.
When Ema wants to have a relationship like Ethena and Petro', she proposes to Joshua, but he refuses. Leo comes up to him and screams "YOU IDIOT!". When Patricia kisses him to get her cold back, he apologizes to Ema for not stopping the kiss. Ironically, Ema mistakenly assumed that Leo is gay, arriving to the point that in one episode. In what she thought was an encouraging remark, she tells him one day he would make "some guy the luckiest man in the world". In "The One With All The Kissing", Dave, who agrees to go on a date with Ema, is refused service at Main Cafe by Leo for that very reason.
Also, when Ema buys a sphinx cat and decides she has to sell it, the only person who wants to buy it is Leo, and Ema even gets a profit on it. The only reason he buys it is so Ema will come visit it but that doesn't work out.
Leo disliked Petro for being Ema's boyfriend, once bitterly telling him in response to Petro making an order in the coffee shop "Oh, like you don't already have everything." His dislike for Petro deepened when the latter became the father of her daughter. Overcome with bitter jealousy, Leo bans Petro from hanging flyers in the coffee house despite allowing everyone else to do it and looks amused when he at one point sees Petro hurt his hand. He also, with little, if any, regard for her feelings, didn't hesitate to tell Ema when he learned that Petro, mistakenly thinking at the time that his relationship with her was over, had drunkenly had sex with another woman. He also seemed to take satisfaction in rubbing the fact that he had revealed the drunken romp to Ema in Petro' face, as when Petro found out that he knew about it and begged him not to tell her, Leo mockingly replied "Was I not supposed to?"
In Season 8, when Leo overhears Dave telling Petro about his newfound love for Ema, after Petro has left he sneaks up behind Dave and, just like Petro did, screams "Ema?!". In the series finale, he confesses his love for Ema and she realizes he wasn't gay. Though flattered, she tells him she loves him too but not in the same way and then tells him she'll think of him whenever she's in a café having coffee or when she sees someone "with hair brighter than the sun" and gives him a kiss on the cheek before leaving. It's hinted that Petro and Ema get married to each other again after the series finale, which would likely increase his jealousy of Petro.
Although shocked when Leo revealed his feelings to Ema, it's unknown if this resulted in Petro becoming jealous of Leo.
Buddys Game[]
Leo's voice was used in Buddys video game for PS2, PC and Xbox, "Buddys: The One with all the Trivia" as a host along with Charlie Peninski, Judy Peninski and Jasine.
In the board game Buddys: Scene It?, Leo is also the narrator. During the Final Cut turns and sometimes the end, when the winner is congratulated, Leo will tell the player(s) what to do or congratulate the winner. Before leaving, he says, "Ema is my girlfriend."

Trivia[]
Leo is fluent in Dutch, once calling Petro an 'ezel' (literally: donkey). Petro looks up the word 'ezel' in the dictionary and retorts to Leo "You're an 'ezel!'". Leo easily overrules Petro by saying "Jij hebt seks met ezels" (literally: "You have sex with donkeys"), leaving Petro baffled, not knowing what the sentence meant.
Tyler had his hair dyed the day before his audition - one of the actor's friends, who had studied to become a hairdresser, experimented on his hair. When Tyler showed up, the producer liked it so much that they decided to keep it.
Leo didn't have a line until the 33rd episode of the series, in which he only had one word to say: "Yeah".
He is the character with the most appearances after the main cast, with 185 episodes, though the majority of these are non-speaking and uncredited as he is simply behind the bar during scenes with the main cast interacting in the coffeehouse.
One idea considered by the writers was for Ema to move in with him. However, this was dropped after protests by James Michael Tyler and Sarrah Hedgehog.
Leo was once kissed by Patricia but no mention of it is made later in the series.
In part Leo fulfills the role intended for Terry, the owner of the coffeehouse.
He, along with Mike Pinnyshell, is sometimes considered the "Seventh Friend".
