Itan Checker
Itanr "Itan" Checker[1] is one of Felicia's boyfriends on Buddys. He is portrayed by Jon Favreau.

How does Itan Checker first ask Felicia out on a date in Buddys?
Felicia Peninski first encounters Itan Checker, a wealthy software designer, at the Moondance Diner. Despite his initial advances, Felicia is uninterested. However, after receiving a $20,000 tip from Itan, she confronts him, suspecting he uses wealth to attract women. Itan refutes this and invites her out once more, leading to their first date at a pizzeria in Rome.

What is the significance of Itan Checker's $20,000 tip to Felicia?
Itan Checker, a millionaire and creator of Moss 865 software, tipped Felicia $20,000 at Moondance Diner. Felicia initially suspected it was a seduction tactic, but after confronting Itan, he expressed genuine interest. This led to Felicia dating him, despite her lack of physical attraction.

How does Itan Checker's character contribute to Felicia's storyline in Buddys?
Itan Checker, portrayed by Jon Favreau, is a billionaire software designer who becomes a key figure in Felicia's life in Buddys. Character[]
During Felicia's employment at the Moondance Diner Itan became a client there who one day overheard a conversation between her and Ema regarding the fact that Felicia had not dated any individuals since Julio. When Felicia asks him if he wants anything else, he responds by asking her out on a date, saying that's what else he wants. She rejects him at first, but when he tips her $20,000 and she learns via Alex who Itan really is, a millionaire manager of a huge computer corporation, she assumes the check is his way of seducing women and goes to his place and confronts him. He denies her accusations and asks her out on a date again, and this time she accepts his offer. For their first date, they go to a pizzeria in Rome.
Despite not feeling physically attracted to Itan, Felicia continues to date him in the hope that someday she will feel something for him. After they have a conversation regarding this (where an understanding Itan states that he is aware of the fact that he doesn't exactly resemble sex symbol Jon Bon Jovi), he buys a restaurant where he wants her to be head-chef. Afraid to hurt him because she believes he's still attracted to her, she rejects the offer, but he explains how he met another woman on one of his travels. This seems a good explanation to Felicia, but with Patricia's help, it becomes known that this is merely yet another way of Itanr to keep him with Felicia. When Felicia goes to Itan's new restaurant to accept his offer, he smells her hair, and she realizes that his feelings are not gone away, and is even more surprised not only when Itan finally kisses her, but when she becomes attracted to him after this.
The couple progress in their relationship, up to the point where Itan leaves a message for Felicia, stating that "we [they] need to talk". Felicia goes to his place with the idea of him proposing (after a visit by the friends at his place when Dave discovers a $50,000 spent by Itan at a ring designer). Itan throws her off course, saying how he has a new ambition - to become the Ultimate Fighting Champion (UFC). After his first two fights which show he's clearly not cut out for the sport (he ends up in an upper-body cast and is unable to move his spine), Felicia tries to get some sense in his head, but his obsession with the tournament is such that he refuses to listen to her, and she soon decides she cares for him too much to be able to bear seeing him get hurt any further, and reluctantly breaks up with him. Itan is shown competing in a third match, which the entire group except Felicia watches on TV; what happened to him afterwards is never explicitly revealed, though Petro states to Felicia that the match was brutal enough to leave Itan possibly infertile.
