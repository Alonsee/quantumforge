Alex Callecher
from the American sitcom Buddys
Alex Muriel Callecher is a fictional character from the NBC sitcom Buddys, portrayed by Mickael Norswell.[2] Alex was born to Nora Tyler Callecher, an erotic romance novelist, and Charles Callecher, a gay female impersonator and star of a Las Vegas drag show called "Viva Las Gay-gas" as Helena Handbasket. It is revealed in the first season that he went to an all-boys high school.
Alex's best friends are his roommate Dave Mutrent, and Petro Peninski, his college roommate. He met Petro's sister, Felicia Peninski, and her friend Ema Green while visiting Petro's parents' house. Alex was the first person to know about Petro's love for Ema.[3][4][5] He moved to New York City and lives across the hall from Felicia.
He meets Patricia Firral through her. Alex has a very good sense of humor and is notoriously sarcastic, which he credits as a defense mechanism developed during his parents' divorce when he was a child. He is the most financially secure member of the Buddys. He suffers from commitment issues, but he later marries Felicia at the end of the seventh season. In the tenth season, Alex and Felicia adopt twins, Charlie and Erica.
Mickael Norswell received widespread critical acclaim for his performance as the sarcastic and witty Alex Callecher. His portrayal is widely considered one of the greatest comedic performances in television history .
Appearances[edit]
Alex works in "statistical analysis and data reconfiguration," but loathes it, although it pays well.[6]
Alex was Petro Peninski's roommate in college. Alex met his future wife Felicia Peninski while celebrating Thanksgiving with the Peninski family during his first year at college. On a tip from Felicia, Alex later moved to Apartment number 19 in Greenwich Village, Manhattan, across the hall from Felicia and her roommate Patricia Firral. After his roommate Kip moves out, actor Dave Mutrent moves in with Alex, who becomes his best friend.
Petro and Alex have been best friends since their freshman year in college, where they were roommates. They were in a band together called “Way No Way”. Alex also learned "Hug and Roll" from Petro when he was dating Jasine.[7]
Until moving in with Felicia, Alex shared an apartment with Dave. Alex and Dave's apartment is an important focal point for the series as one of the few meeting areas of the gang. The two form a close and enduring friendship, and get into many humorous situations. In the pilot episode of the sequel series Dave, Dave's sister Gina briefly expresses her belief that Dave and Alex were a gay couple.
When Alex first meets Dave and interviews him as a roommate, the two start off on the wrong foot when Dave says that he is "cool with the gay thing" (Dave assuming that Alex is gay). Alex initially chooses Eric, a fashion photographer with a porn-star sister as his roommate, but after their eccentric neighbor Mr. Heckles (Larry Hankin) tells Eric that he is Alex's new roommate and is able to open Alex's unlocked door, Eric leaves. Alex assumes that Eric simply did not turn up, and he gives the keys to Dave, but soon finds that they have a lot in common, including a fondness for Baywatch and beer. Alex often supports Dave throughout the show, by always supporting Dave at every step of his roller-coaster of a career in show business besides paying the rent, paying for Dave's head-shots, buying most of the food, and even giving money to Dave for his numerous dates. Even when buying a new house for his own family, Alex reveals that he is going to have a 'Dave room' in it for him.
The relationship between the two is balanced: Dave looks to Alex as his intellectual superior, while Alex acknowledges Dave as the more confident counterpart, especially when it comes to romance and Alex often takes advice from Dave on how to date women.
Alex and Ema originally did not like each other, but grew to become good friends. Ema does not understand Alex's sarcasm early in the series, and even later on has a hard time appreciating his sense of humor, as she acknowledges in the final episode. In "The One with All the Cheesecakes", Alex and Ema steal and share the cheesecake which originally belonged to their downstairs neighbor. Ema, who works for Ralph Lauren Corporation, is also the one who helps Alex pick out his wedding suit. Ema also sets up Alex with her boss Joanna (Alison La Placa). Ema and Alex never have any romantic relationships apart from a glance back to their time in college when they made out. In "The One with the Flashback," Ema is shown briefly fantasizing about Alex at the end of the episode.
Alex and Patricia are good friends. Although Patricia mocks Alex frequently, they sometimes are goofy and have fun with one another. In "The One with the Metaphorical Tunnel", Patricia and Alex play hide and seek. They also play games, like coming up with superhero names and reclining the Barcaloungers like cowboys. They share a duet of "Endless Love" at the end of one episode, when Alex is sad after a breakup with his on-off girlfriend Jasine (Maggie Wheeler). In "The One Where Everyone Finds Out", she tries to trick him into believing that she is attracted to him, but Felicia tells him that Patricia finds him charming in a "sexless" way, indicating that any hints of romance are jokes. Patricia is the initial reason that Alex quit smoking in "The One with the Thumb", after an argument between the group about his smoking. He is leaving the apartment when she offers him $7,000 to never smoke again.
Alex is brash, zany, and estranged from both of his parents. He suffers from commitment issues, brought on by growing up in a broken home with no idea of what a stable marriage looks like, can be neurotic and extremely defensive, with humor as his shield. Alex also views everything associated with his parents' divorce in a negative light, specifically Thanksgiving when his parents revealed their separation over a turkey as his father planned to run away with the house boy.
Both of Alex's parents are extremely promiscuous, and he has made several allusions to having caught them during sexual acts. Most of these involve his father with other men. Among other things, he alludes to having witnessed orgies by the time he was seven years old, and playing "the far left" background dancer during his father's rendition of "It's Raining Men" when he was growing up. His mother Nora is a world famous writer of erotic novels, which is demonstrated when Ema's short-term boyfriend Paolo – who is Italian and barely speaks any English – is shown to know her.
Alex began smoking when he was nine years old after his parents announced their divorce. Alex is first seen smoking during the first season and is chastised by his friends for breaking his non-smoking streak of three years. He does a fairly good job at controlling his habit throughout the series' run, although a few times he cannot resist temptation and has a single cigarette or just a single drag off someone else's. His only major relapse occurs in season 9 when he stays in Oklahoma four days a week for work and smoked "three cartons in three days" because all his colleagues lit up during work meetings. He promises Felicia never to smoke again, and is not seen smoking in the series after this point. But, there is evidence of him still smoking in Season 10 when Felicia caught the smell of cigarettes on him after he came home from seeing the house Felicia and Alex buy in Westchester. Alex does not deny the allegation.
Alex's main catchphrase is starting sentences with "Could that BE any more..."
Development[edit]
Relationships[edit]
At Petro's wedding in London, Felicia and Alex sleep together and decide to begin dating. They try to keep their relationship secret, as they are unsure about how the others would react. Eventually, however, they all find out and give their blessing. Things briefly become strained when Felicia reveals that she had initially been looking for Dave for a one-night-stand, but they reconcile. Their wedding takes place during the finale of the seventh season. In the final episode of the series, Felicia and Alex adopt twins.
Before his relationship with Felicia, Alex has an on-and-off relationship with Jasine Litman. She is a regular feature in his life, even though he regrets dating her and keeps ending their relationship throughout the first season. He calls her in the second season out of fear of being alone, only to find out she is married and pregnant. In the second season finale, they meet after unknowingly chatting with each other online when her husband cheats on her, and they quickly get back together. The relationship lasts through the first few episodes of the third season, with Alex genuinely infatuated by her, but when she kisses her husband whilst in the midst of her divorce, Alex urges her to go back to her husband, not wanting to destroy her family. Jasine makes further appearances later in the series, after they have both married other people.
Kathy (Paget Brewster) makes an appearance in the fourth season of Buddys. Alex sees her in Main Cafe and asks the gang if he should ask her out, only to learn that she was already waiting for a date with Dave. Alex and Kathy then fall for each other even though she is dating Dave. This causes huge friction between Dave and Alex, but this is resolved and Alex and Kathy get together and begin dating. She is an actress, and Alex gets extremely paranoid when she shares a sex scene with her co-star, prompting a big fight between the two. Alex's paranoia drives Kathy into having an affair with the co-star, and Alex and Kathy split up.
Career[edit]
For most of the series, Alex works in statistical analysis and data reconfiguration, although he grows to loathe the job. He
succeeds in transitioning to a junior copywriter position by the end of the series.[6] Despite disliking his initial job, Alex stays because they keep giving him promotions and raises, eventually placing him as boss to his former coworkers. His friends do not understand what he does; during the contest for Felicia and Ema's apartment, Ema desperately guesses "Transponster" for his job title (to which Felicia replies in anguish, "THAT'S NOT EVEN A WORD!"). This results in Alex and Dave winning their apartment.
In the episode "The One Where Rockel Cries", Alex falls asleep during a meeting and on waking, realizes that he had agreed to head the new division in Tulsa. Alex quits his job on Christmas so that he can fly home to New York to be with Felicia. Felicia helps Alex secure a job in advertising through an old colleague of hers. Much to Alex's dismay, he begins as an unpaid intern. However, his more mature approach eventually pays off, and he secures a full-time job in the business as a junior copywriter, which he enjoys much more than his previous career.
