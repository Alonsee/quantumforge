History[]
Kinoa was Kranny's former home economics high school teacher. She claims that he was her best student. He claims that she was his best teacher. Although they both claim to have pure love, they lust for each other easily, including in public places such as coffee houses or hospitals.
Patricia disapproved of their relationship due to the age gap between the two (26 years), and bemused them both with her daft comments during her attempts to split them up. Eventually however, she came to accept their relationship.
In the show she is only referred to as Kinoa Allicon. After she and Kranny Firral Jr. married, it is unknown if Kinoa kept her last name, or legally changed it to Firral or Allicon-Firral.
She and Kranny cannot conceive due to Kinoa's age, so they ask Patricia to carry the embryo, which she agrees to do. In "The One Hundredth", Patricia had triplets: Leslie, Kranny Jr. Jr., and Alex.
Age[]
In "The One With The Hypnosis Tape", it’s said that she was 44 and her fiancé was 18. Based upon that episode, which takes place in early 1997, she was born c. October 1952, meaning that she would be 45 later that year, and her fiancé would be born October 25, 1978.
