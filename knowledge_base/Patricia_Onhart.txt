History[]
Patricia Onhart is only seen once, when she dies and Dr. Drake Ramoray, played by Dave, gets her brain, so he can awake from his coma, a procedure Petro takes issue with on scientific grounds.
She was famous for throwing drinks at people and slapping them. Her daughter, named Dina, was seeing a man named Frederick, which Patricia did not approve. She was thrown off a horse and onto an electric fence, an accident set up by Frederick and Dina. After handing over the role of Patricia Onhart to Dave, Cecilia Monroe slept with Dave causing minor hysteria for Ema and Felicia, who both are big fans of Days of our Lives.
Later scenes exploring Dave's role in the show as Patricia in Drake Ramoray's body. In "The One With Ema's Date", it is revealed Drake's body is rejecting her brain, and they need to inject fluid from her dead body. Drake is told, however, that Patricia's body is mysteriously missing.
Trivia[]
Cecilia has played "Patricia" for many seasons of Days of Our Lives.
Patricia had a daughter, Dina, with whom she had a bad relationship. Dina killed her mother to run off with her money and her boyfriend, of whom Patricia didn't approve, who helped execute the murder.
Later, however, her body is missing. This suggests she is either still alive (though without a brain), or dug up by Dina and Frederick.
